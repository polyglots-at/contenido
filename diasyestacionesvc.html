🏠 5. Casos y Situaciones

(Ái góu tu guórk from Món-dei tu Frái-dei)

(Ái rest on Sá-tur-dei and Sán-dei)

In summer, I go to the beach.
(In Sá-mer, ái góu tu de biich)

(In Uín-ter, it is kóuld)

(On Sán-dei, ái ví-sit mái gránd-má-der)
